How to view

Parallel (Magic Eye): keep the picture at arm's length and look "through" the screen as if at something far behind it, so the two guide dots turn into three. Hold your gaze softly and the 3D shape appears. If it will not settle, make the image smaller on screen or change the strip width.

Cross-eye: cross your eyes slightly so the two pictures overlap into a third one in the middle.
