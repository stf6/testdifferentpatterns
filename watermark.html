Video Watermark Remover

Hides a small mark (a logo, a corner stamp, a text label) in a video you made, by filling the area from its surroundings, or by cropping it out. It all runs in your browser: your video never leaves your device.

1. Video

Choose a video or GIF.

2. Mark the watermark

Drag on the picture to draw a box around it, or start from a corner. Keep the box as tight as you can.

3. How to remove it

Only fills the pixels that stay still while the video moves behind them, so less of the picture is touched. Works on moving footage only, and can leave faint traces on soft glows. If you see traces, switch it off.

4. Output

Good to know

Fill works best on small marks over smooth or moving backgrounds. It cannot bring back what was hidden: on a large mark over fine detail you will see a soft patch. Crop is exact, so use it when the mark sits near an edge. It is the safest choice for big marks.

Only use this on videos you made or have the right to edit. It hides the visible mark only. It does not touch invisible watermarks (such as SynthID) that some AI tools embed, and it is not meant to hide that a video is AI-generated, so say so where that matters.

Speed: the fill itself takes about a millisecond per frame. Most of the time goes on reading and re-encoding the video. Keep the tab open until it finishes. Needs Chrome, Edge or Brave to save MP4 video.
